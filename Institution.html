Institutional Overview

Sanskrithi School of Engineering (SSE), Puttaparthi, established in 2015 under the Sanskrithi Group of Institutions, is an autonomous engineering college dedicated to empowering students with practical skills, innovative mindsets, and sustainable engineering principles.
