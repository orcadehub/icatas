Conference Mission & Vision

The 3rd International Conference on Advances in Technologies and Applications for Sustainability (ICATAS 2026) brings together leading researchers, academicians, industry professionals, and policy makers from across the globe to foster interdisciplinary collaboration and advance technologies that directly contribute to the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs).
