About Conference

Global Platform for Sustainable Innovations

The International Conference on Advances in Technologies and Applications for Sustainability (ICATAS - 2026) is intended to provide a premier interdisciplinary forum for researchers, engineers and academicians to present and discuss the most recent trends, innovations, concerns, technologies, practical challenges and applications adopted for Sustainable development.

The conference provides an enduring techno-platform for scientists, scholars, engineers, and students from universities all around the world and industry to present ongoing research activities focused on solutions to achieve Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs).

3rd ICATAS 2026 Journal Pathway

Extension to Scopus-Indexed Pertanika Journal

Selected papers may be invited for extension as full-length papers for consideration in the Scopus-indexed Pertanika Journal of Science & Technology, published by Universiti Putra Malaysia (UPM). Extended papers will be subject to the journal’s standard peer-review and acceptance process and will require payment of an additional Article Processing Charge (APC), as applicable.

Mandatory Requirement for Special Issue Authors: Authors submitting papers to the Special Issue are required to first submit their papers to Pertanika Proceedings. Only after fulfilling the proceedings submission and publication requirements may the papers be considered for extension and submission to the Pertanika Journal of Science & Technology.
