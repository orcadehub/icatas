Paper Submission Guidelines

Submit Your Original Research Paper to ICATAS 2026

Submission Process & Policies

Originality & Plagiarism

All submitted papers must represent original, unpublished work. Papers undergoing review at other conferences or journals will not be accepted. Submissions will be screened for plagiarism using standard tools.

Paper Format

Manuscripts must be formatted strictly according to the official Word or LaTeX templates provided on the Publication page (up to 7 pages including figures and references).
