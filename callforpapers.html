Submit Your Innovative Research Work in Sustainable Engineering & Tech

Conference Scope & Call for Contributions

3rd ICATAS 2026 has been widely deployed to domains such as Innovative Technologies and applications for Sustainable Engineering, Sustainable Science, Sustainable Society and Living, Energy Engineering, Material Sciences, Sustainable Environment et al.

Prospective authors are kindly encouraged to contribute high quality research papers describing original and unpublished results of conceptual, constructive, empirical, experimental, or theoretical work in all areas of Sustainability.
